About

The guy behind the terminal.

I'm an IT professional by day, a problem solver by default, and occasionally the reason someone says, “Have you tried turning it off and on again?”

My journey started with a degree in Computer Applications and led me into IT support and system administration. Since then, I've been learning how the pieces fit together, from operating systems and networks to the infrastructure that keeps everything running. I like figuring out why things break, finding the fix, and understanding enough to hopefully stop the same problem from coming back.

I'm also exploring cloud infrastructure and automation, because apparently knowing how to fix one thing just leads to discovering ten more things that can break. If it works, don't touch it. Unless, of course, you're in a test environment. Then touch everything. That's how you learn.

Outside the IT universe, it's a different story. Gaming, football, spontaneous plans, road trips with the boys, and the occasional adventure that sounded like a better idea in the group chat. I'm all for good company, questionable plans, and stories that get funnier every time they're retold.

Curious enough to keep learning, competitive enough to hate losing, and sensible enough to know when to leave a working system alone.

Mostly sensible, anyway.
