Experience / WebCastle Media and Zoople Technologies

WebCastle Media and Zoople Technologies

  • Senior IT Administrator
  • Jan 2025 – Jul 2026
  • Kochi, Kerala

I was the lead IT administrator for WebCastle Media and its subsidiary, Zoople Technologies, an IT training institute. I looked after both at once.

What I handled

  • Servers, routers, switches, firewalls and access points across both organizations.
  • Network design: LAN, VLANs, subnetting, routing and access control.
  • User accounts, permissions and sign-in across Windows Active Directory and Linux.
  • Windows Server and Linux systems: hardening, patching, service setup and monitoring.
  • More than 200 workstations and laptops: setup, updates and software compliance.
  • IT assets from purchase to retirement, including vendor talks, billing and licenses.
  • Documentation: network diagrams, asset registers, procedures and incident logs.
  • A team of two junior IT staff, whom I led and coached.

Stories from the job

Network design

Replacing an unplanned network with a segmented one

ChallengeThe office ran on a mix of mismatched switches and modems with no network plan, which made traffic hard to control, secure and troubleshoot.

ApproachMade the case for a proper router and new access points, then designed and set up separate VLANs for the wired network and the Wi-Fi, and recorded the design in network diagrams.

OutcomeA planned, segmented LAN and Wi-Fi that was easier to manage, secure and support.

Connectivity

Keeping the business online through ISP outages

ChallengeRepeated outages and IP address changes from the internet provider were interrupting work.

ApproachMoved the office onto the secondary connection to keep it running, then worked with the provider’s technical team and the router vendor’s support team to resolve the problem and reconfigure the setup.

OutcomeWork carried on over the secondary link while the primary was fixed, and the configuration was updated to cope with provider changes.

New office

Planning the network for a new office

ChallengeA newly opened office needed its network planned from scratch, and it was in a different location.

ApproachTravelled to the site to plan the network side of the setup in person.

OutcomeThe new office had a planned network to build from.
